🚀 Welcome to the AI Interview Evaluator

How It Works:

  • 📄 Upload your Resume and optional Job Description.
  • 🧠 Our AI generates personalized interview questions.
  • 🎤 Answer them via text or speech.
  • 📹 Webcam + timer run during the session.
  • 🧮 Answers are evaluated using STAR, Bloom’s Taxonomy, CEFR, and heuristic scoring.
  • 📊 You’ll receive a structured feedback report.
